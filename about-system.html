About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The system turns raw temperature readings into clear, timely warnings. It aims to give every district at least 24 hours of notice before dangerous heat arrives.

Data Sources

Forecasts combine readings from automatic weather stations, records held by the India Meteorological Department and live satellite imagery. Each reading is checked before it reaches the alert engine.

Stakeholders

The portal serves citizens, district officers, hospitals and forecasters. Each group sees what it needs and can take the actions that matter for its role.

Heat kills quietly. Our job is to make sure no family learns about a heatwave only after it has arrived.

Dr. A. Kulkarni, Chief Meteorologist
