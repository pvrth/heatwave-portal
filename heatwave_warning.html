Decision rules

Rules applied in order
ConditionRecommendation
Temperature ≥ 40°CSevere Heatwave Warning
Temperature ≥ 38°C and Humidity ≥ 60%High Heat Risk – Issue Warning

Forecast temperature ≥ 40°CEarly Warning – Prepare for Heatwave
