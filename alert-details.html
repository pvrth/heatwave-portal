Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin

Severe Heatwave Alert for North Zone. Issued by the IMD on 12th May 2026, valid until 15th May 2026. Bulletin version 2.1.

Conditions

Maximum temperature is expected to reach 47°C, with a heat index of up to 52°C.

A SHW is declared when an AWS records 45°C or more, or when readings stay 6.5°C above normal. A regular HW begins at 40°C.

Advisory

Themes for this bulletin: stay hydrated, avoid outdoor work from 12 noon to 4 pm and check on elderly neighbours.

Drink at least 3 L of water (H2O) a day, even when you do not feel thirsty.

Forecast subject to revision. Always follow the latest bulletin from the IMD.
