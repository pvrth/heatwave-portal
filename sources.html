Data Sources and Stakeholders

Data sources

  • AWS sensors
  • IMD database
  • Satellite feeds

Forecast and alert workflow

  1. Collect readings from stations and satellites.
  2. Validate and clean the data.
  3. Run the heatwave forecast model.
  4. Classify severity for each region.
  5. Issue the alert bulletin.
  6. Send advisories to subscribers.

Stakeholders and their actions

  • Citizens
    • View alerts and forecasts
    • Subscribe to advisories
  • District officers
    • Open cooling centres
    • Send local instructions
  • Hospitals
    • Prepare for heatstroke cases
    • Report patient numbers
  • Forecasters
    • Review model output
    • Approve and publish bulletins

Abbreviations

AWS
Automatic Weather Station. A device that measures temperature, humidity and wind.
IMD
India Meteorological Department. The national weather agency.
HW
Heatwave. A spell of unusually high temperature.
SHW
Severe Heatwave. A heatwave at or above 45°C, or far above normal.
